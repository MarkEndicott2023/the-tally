Sessions

A rep is one prediction-error cycle: attempt or predict, get the verdict, take the feedback. Every click is one point. The session closes when points reach the threshold; keep clicking past it if you like. Label names the session (optimization, big data, python…) and is filed with it; click a filed label to rename it, click its date to change it, ✕ deletes the session. A session is dated by its first rep, so a page left open for days files under the day the reps happened. Minutes is optional: type how long the session ran and it is filed with it; click a filed session's minutes to set or change them. New session files the current count and resets to zero. Streak is dailyish: it counts days with 2 or more reps, summed across that day's sessions and the live one, and forgives a single day off between them; two days off in a row break it. The threshold plays no part. Gold is intact, ice means the chain is alive and today is still open (the label says when today is the last chance), 0 means the chain is broken. Best is the longest streak on record; it turns gold when the live streak is the record. Save links the page to a tally.json you choose: it reads what the file already holds, merges, writes, and from then on every change goes to the file, deletes included.
